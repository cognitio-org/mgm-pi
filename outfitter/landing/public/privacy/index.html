Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

In short: we collect what we need to run Kernal and Outfitter for invited people: your Google email, what you and your agents do in Outfitter, and the keys you choose to store. We don't sell personal data, don't run advertising or analytics trackers, and send data to other companies only to provide the service (the cloud, the database and the AI models your agents use).

2. What this policy covers

  • the website kernal.one;
  • Outfitter, the web app at app.kernal.one and its API (link.kernal.one), including agent sandboxes (“Drives”) and preview links on kernal.run;
  • Kernal, the coding agent you run on your own computer, where it connects to our services.

Outfitter is currently available by invitation only.

3. What we collect

Account and sign-in

  • Your email address, from Google sign-in. We don't receive your Google password and we don't store your Google account ID.
  • When you sign Kernal in on a computer: a device record (the device's name and platform, and the Kernal version). Device and API tokens are stored only as one-way hashes; you can see and revoke them.
  • Optional profile details an administrator may add for commit attribution: display name, GitHub username and GitHub no-reply email.
  • Your settings and named profiles (for example your default model or voice preferences).

What you and your agents do

  • Agent sessions: your prompts, the agents' replies, tool calls and their results, files and code the agents read or write in their sandboxes, messages between agents, and browser captures you request. This can include anything you put in your repositories or prompts, so please don't paste data you aren't allowed to share.
  • Search index: sessions are split into chunks for search. Secret-looking strings are redacted before indexing.
  • Agent memory (where enabled): facts the agents keep between sessions. On a laptop, memory recording is off unless you turn it on.
  • Repository access: agents work in GitHub repositories you connect, through a GitHub App or a token you provide.

Keys and secrets you give us

  • Personal provider API keys (for example an OpenRouter key) you choose to save to your account. They are stored in Google Secret Manager, delivered only to your own signed-in devices, and not shown again in the web app.
  • Keys kept only in Kernal on your computer (its .env file) stay on your computer.

Usage and cost

  • AI model usage and cost per person and per agent: model names, token counts, spend and budgets. Your router key is linked to your email so spend can be attributed to you.
  • Voice usage totals, under a pseudonymous ID derived from your email.

Voice

If you use voice input, audio streams from your browser or computer directly to our speech-to-text provider; we don't store the audio.

Technical data

  • Server logs kept by our cloud provider (for example request times, paths and IP addresses) for security and operations.
  • Error reports, only if error reporting is switched on (it is off by default). Request bodies and headers are removed, and Kernal strips secrets from errors before sending. If an administrator turns on agent tracing, prompts and outputs may be included in redacted form. On the web app, an error may include a replay of the page session with text and inputs masked.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

  • Google sign-in cookies, set by Google's Identity-Aware Proxy, keep you signed in to the web app. They are strictly necessary.
  • kernal_welcome: your first name, so kernal.one can greet you (180 days).
  • Local storage in your browser for preferences: theme, sound, the BASIC programs you save on kernal.one, tour progress and similar. These never leave your browser.

7. International transfers

We host in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

8. How long we keep it

  • Sessions are kept until you delete them. Deleting (“formatting”) a session erases its transcript, runs, search index, memory sessions and browser data. A small record that it was deleted, and by whom, remains.
  • Messages between agents and short-lived approvals expire automatically.
  • Your account, settings, keys and usage records are kept while you have access. When access ends, or when you ask, we delete or anonymise them within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.
  • Logs and error reports are kept for the providers' standard periods (typically 30 to 90 days).

9. Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS only). The web app sits behind Google sign-in with an allow-list. Secrets live in Secret Manager and reach sandboxes over authenticated connections, never in plain configuration. Tokens are stored hashed, and each agent sandbox is isolated. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell you without undue delay if a breach affects your data.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to [privacy@ contact email]; we answer within one month. You can also delete sessions and revoke devices and tokens yourself in the web app. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may complain to your data protection authority.

California residents: we don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

11. Children
