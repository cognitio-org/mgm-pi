# Style bible: "Dear Sysop" (early-90s corporate promo)

Distilled from ~15 reference frames of the source promo (kept local, faces pixelated). This
describes the look and the devices; it does not reuse the source's people, names, hardware or logos.
Everything under "Prompt suffix" goes on every generation prompt.

## Format
- 4:3, standard-definition tape dub; soft, low resolution, chroma smear, highlights bloom.
- 29.97 fps. Mostly locked-off or slow push-ins; one handheld exterior (kids running).
- The YouTube copy's 4px scanlines are a transfer artifact, not the look. Real scanlines appear
  only on *macro shots of a CRT* (see "CRT macro").

## Color and contrast (measured)
- Live action: low saturation (mean chroma ~28/255), lifted blacks (2nd-percentile luma 40-65),
  highlights capped ~210. Warm-pink skin; R > G ~= B overall.
- Interior palette: **ochre / mustard-yellow walls**, **powder-blue** polos and partitions,
  **beige / platinum** plastics, light-wood desks. Accents: green polo, red mailbox flag.
- Graphics and "fantasy" shots go pastel and brighter: lavender sea (#bcbcfe), pale aqua sky,
  white glow; near-white backgrounds with only the text dark (printout, wish card).
- Exterior: overcast late afternoon, desaturated greens, beige stucco suburb.

## Shot vocabulary
1. **Computer lab, three-quarter from behind the monitor**: monitor edge as a big soft foreground
   wedge on frame right, subject mid-frame, ochre wall with posters behind. The hero framing.
2. **Side-by-side at the lab bench**: two kids in profile at beige machines, second CRT behind.
3. **Overhead insert**: hand on a one-button-style mouse, corner of a beige keyboard, light wood.
4. **CRT macro**: extreme close-up of a menu on a blue screen, visible scanlines and glow, the
   highlighted row bright white. (Outfitter's own GEOS-style menus fit this exactly.)
5. **Dot-matrix printout**: slow pan down tractor-feed paper (sprocket holes both sides), a
   bulleted wish list in a dot-matrix face, dark room behind.
6. **Wish card**: a crayon drawing on off-white paper, a quote in big white serif with a grey
   drop shadow/emboss over it, a blue-ink signature, and "Age N" bottom right.
7. **Thought glow**: a computer floating inside a soft white elliptical glow above a head, plain
   pale blue-grey backdrop.
8. **Paint-program fantasy**: 1-bit-era pixel-art scene (beach, sun, dolphins, crab) with live
   video composited inside the computer's screen.
9. **Mailbox**: the envelope close-up (hand-lettered address, a stamp) going into a curbside
   mailbox; and a handheld shot of kids running down a suburban sidewalk toward the mailbox.
10. **Signature over action**: a cursive signature in white with a chrome/purple outline,
    written on over the hero shot.

## Editing
- Slow cross-dissolves (1-1.5 s) almost everywhere; montage beats use 2-3 layer double exposures
  (`blend=all_mode=screen` ~40%) and glowing horizontal title bands sliding across.
- The arc: kids' letters/wishes -> mailed to the company -> answered by the product -> the
  thumbs-up.

## Performance beats (hero)
- Reads the letter flat and earnest; slow approving nod at the screen; turns to camera; a single
  thumbs-up; hold 1 s; freeze frame; the signature writes on.

## Prompt suffix
> 1990 corporate promotional video shot on Betacam and dubbed to VHS, 4:3 standard definition,
> soft and slightly blurry, low saturation, lifted blacks, warm skin, mild tape grain and chroma
> bleed, mustard-yellow walls, powder-blue and beige tones, generic unbranded beige desktop
> computer with separate CRT monitor, no logos, no brand names, no text unless specified.
