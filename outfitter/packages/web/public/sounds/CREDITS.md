landing page (`outfitter/landing/public/sounds/`). Short excerpts (trimmed, mixed to mono,
high-passed at 60 Hz, peak-normalized) from two freesound_community recordings on Pixabay,
distributed under the Pixabay Content License: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/

- "Floppy drive on old PC" (Pixabay item 52014)
- "Reading floppy disc 2" (Pixabay item 35303)

launch.mp3: 52014 at 125.15-127.45 s (head seek) + 35303 at 1.0-3.4 s (reading, +12 dB)
